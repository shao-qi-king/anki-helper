# 英语语法完整手册（口语导向）

> 目标：以沟通为主，从句子最基本的结构开始，逐步覆盖日常口语所需的全部语法。
> 每个语法点都配有"什么时候用"和大量例句，方便直接录入 Anki 练习。

---

## 第一部分：句子的骨架

### 1. 五种基本句型

英语所有句子都由这五种结构变化而来：

| 句型 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 主 + 谓 | S + V | Birds fly. / I slept. |
| 主 + 谓 + 宾 | S + V + O | I like music. / She speaks English. |
| 主 + 系 + 表 | S + V + C | He is tall. / The food smells good. |
| 主 + 谓 + 间宾 + 直宾 | S + V + O1 + O2 | He gave me a book. / She told him a story. |
| 主 + 谓 + 宾 + 宾补 | S + V + O + C | They made me happy. / We call him Tom. |

**名词解释：**
- 主语 (Subject)：动作的发出者 → "谁"
- 谓语 (Verb)：动作本身 → "做什么"
- 宾语 (Object)：动作的承受者 → "对谁/什么做"
- 表语 (Complement)：描述主语的状态 → "是什么样的"
- 补语 (Object Complement)：补充说明宾语 → "让宾语怎么了"

### 2. 句子成分详解

**主语 — 谁在做？**
- 名词：Tom likes dogs.
- 代词：He is my friend.
- 动名词：Swimming is fun.
- 不定式：To learn English is important.
- it 作形式主语：It is important to learn English.（口语更常用这种）

**谓语 — 做什么？**
- 实义动词：I eat breakfast at 8.
- 系动词（be / look / feel / sound / smell / taste / seem / become）：She looks happy.
- 助动词 + 实义动词：I don't know. / Do you like it? / I have finished.
- 情态动词 + 动词原形：I can help you. / You should rest.

**宾语 — 对谁/什么做？**
- 名词：I bought a phone.
- 代词：I love her.
- 动名词：I enjoy reading.
- 不定式：I want to go home.
- 从句：I know (that) he is busy.

**定语 — 修饰名词（什么样的？）**
- 形容词：a beautiful day
- 名词修饰名词：a coffee shop / a bus stop
- 介词短语：the book on the table
- 定语从句：the man who called you

**状语 — 修饰动词/整个句子（何时？何地？如何？为什么？）**
- 时间：I go to bed at 11. / yesterday / every day
- 地点：She works here. / at home / in Beijing
- 方式：He speaks slowly. / with confidence
- 频率：I always drink coffee.
- 原因：I stayed home because of the rain.

### 3. 词序规则

**陈述句标准词序：**
```
主语 + (频率副词) + 谓语 + 宾语 + 方式 + 地点 + 时间
```
- I usually have lunch at the office at noon.
- She quietly read a book in the library yesterday.

**口语中时间/地点可以放句首：**
- Yesterday I went to a movie.
- In China, people eat rice every day.

## 第二部分：时态（什么时候发生）

### 4. 一般现在时

**什么时候用：** 习惯、事实、规律

**构成：** 主语 + 动词原形（第三人称单数加 s/es）

| 场景 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 习惯 | I drink coffee every morning. |
| 事实 | Water boils at 100 degrees. |
| 规律 | The sun rises in the east. |
| 状态描述 | She lives in Shanghai. |
| 频率 | He always arrives late. |

**否定和疑问：**
- I don't like spicy food.
- Does she speak English?
- Where do you work?

**注意：** 第三人称单数 (he/she/it) 用 does，动词回到原形：
- ✓ Does he like coffee?
- ✗ ~~Does he likes coffee?~~

---

### 5. 现在进行时

**什么时候用：** 此刻正在做、近期安排

**构成：** 主语 + am/is/are + 动词ing

| 场景 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 正在做 | I am eating lunch right now. |
| 近期安排 | I am meeting him tomorrow. |
| 暂时状态 | She is staying with her parents this week. |
| 抱怨（always） | He is always complaining! |

**不能用进行时的动词（状态动词）：**
- know, like, love, want, need, believe, understand, remember
- ✗ ~~I am knowing him.~~ → ✓ I know him.

---

### 6. 一般过去时

**什么时候用：** 过去发生且已结束的事

**构成：** 主语 + 动词过去式

| 场景 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 过去的动作 | I went to the gym yesterday. |
| 过去的状态 | She was very tired last night. |
| 连续叙述 | I woke up, took a shower, and left. |

**否定和疑问：**
- I didn't go to work yesterday.
- Did you see the movie?
- What did she say?

**常见不规则动词：**
go→went, see→saw, eat→ate, come→came, take→took, give→gave, know→knew, think→thought, say→said, make→made, get→got

---

### 7. 一般将来时

**什么时候用：** 将要发生的事、计划、临时决定

**三种常用表达：**

| 形式 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| will + 动词 | 临时决定、预测、承诺 | I will help you. / It will rain tomorrow. |
| be going to + 动词 | 已有计划、有迹象 | I am going to visit my parents this weekend. |
| 现在进行时 | 确定的安排 | I am flying to Beijing next Monday. |

**口语中的区别：**
- 有人敲门 → I will get it.（临时决定）
- 买好机票了 → I am going to Japan next month.（计划好了）

---

### 8. 现在完成时

**什么时候用：** 过去发生但和现在有关系（经历、结果、持续）

**构成：** 主语 + have/has + 过去分词

| 场景 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 经历 | I have been to Japan twice. |
| 结果影响现在 | I have lost my key.（所以现在进不去） |
| 从过去到现在 | I have lived here for 5 years. |
| 刚刚完成 | I have just finished dinner. |

**和一般过去时的区别：**
- I lost my key yesterday.（强调过去那个时间点）
- I have lost my key.（强调现在的结果——我现在没钥匙）

**标志词：**
- already, yet, just, ever, never, for, since, so far

---

### 9. 过去进行时

**什么时候用：** 过去某个时刻正在进行的动作（常做背景叙述）

**构成：** 主语 + was/were + 动词ing

| 场景 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 过去某刻在做 | I was sleeping at 10pm. |
| 被打断 | I was cooking when he called. |
| 两个同时进行 | She was reading while I was working. |
| 背景描述 | The sun was shining and birds were singing. |

---

### 10. 过去完成时

**什么时候用：** 过去的过去（两件过去的事，先发生的那件）

**构成：** 主语 + had + 过去分词

| 场景 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 先后顺序 | When I arrived, she had already left. |
| 过去的经历 | I had never seen snow before I moved to Beijing. |
| 后悔 | I wish I had studied harder. |

**口语中常用缩写：** I'd = I had / She'd = She had

---

### 11. 将来进行时 & 将来完成时

**将来进行时**（将来某刻正在做）：
- This time tomorrow, I will be sitting on the plane.
- Don't call at 8, I will be having dinner.

**将来完成时**（到将来某点已完成）：
- By next year, I will have graduated.
- By Friday, I will have finished this project.

（这两个口语中用得少，了解即可）

## 第三部分：语态和语气

### 12. 被动语态

**什么时候用：** 不知道/不关心谁做的，或强调承受者

**构成：** 主语 + be + 过去分词 (+ by 施动者)

| 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|
| Someone stole my phone. | My phone was stolen. |
| They canceled the meeting. | The meeting was canceled. |
| People speak English worldwide. | English is spoken worldwide. |

**口语中常用的被动：**
- I was born in 1995.
- The problem is solved.
- You are invited to the party.
- It is made in China.

---

### 13. 情态动词

| 情态动词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|----------|------|------|
| can | 能力、请求 | I can swim. / Can you help me? |
| could | 过去能力、礼貌请求 | I could run fast when I was young. / Could you pass the salt? |
| may / might | 可能性、许可 | It may rain. / May I sit here? |
| should | 建议、应该 | You should see a doctor. |
| must | 必须、肯定推测 | I must go now. / He must be tired. |
| have to | 客观必须 | I have to work overtime today. |
| would | 意愿、假设 | I would love to go. / Would you like some tea? |
| would rather | 宁愿 | I would rather stay home. |

**can vs be able to：**
- can 用于现在：I can do it.
- was/were able to 用于过去成功做到：I was able to finish on time.

**must vs have to：**
- must = 主观觉得必须：I must study harder.
- have to = 客观要求：I have to wear a uniform at work.

---

### 14. 虚拟语气

**与现在事实相反：**
- If I had more money, I would travel the world.
- If I were you, I would apologize.（be 动词统一用 were）
- I wish I could speak English fluently.

**与过去事实相反：**
- If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.
- I wish I had taken that job.

**建议/要求（that 从句中动词原形）：**
- I suggest that he go to see a doctor.
- It is important that everyone be on time.

（口语中虚拟语气出现频率不高，但 I wish / If I were you 非常常用）

---

## 第四部分：疑问句和否定句

### 15. 疑问句的类型

**一般疑问句（Yes/No 问题）：**
- Do you like it? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Is she coming? — Yes, she is.
- Can you swim? — Yes, I can.
- Have you eaten? — Not yet.

**特殊疑问句（Wh- 问题）：**

| 疑问词 | 问什么 | 例句 |
|--------|--------|------|
| What | 什么 | What do you do?（你做什么工作） |
| Where | 哪里 | Where do you live? |
| When | 何时 | When did you arrive? |
| Who | 谁 | Who told you that? |
| Why | 为什么 | Why are you late? |
| How | 如何 | How do you get to work? |
| How many | 多少（可数） | How many people came? |
| How much | 多少（不可数/价格） | How much is this? |
| How long | 多久 | How long have you been here? |
| How often | 多频繁 | How often do you exercise? |
| Which | 哪一个 | Which one do you prefer? |

**反义疑问句（口语常用来确认）：**
- You are coming, aren't you?
- She didn't call, did she?
- It's cold today, isn't it?

---

### 16. 否定句

**基本否定：**
- I don't know. / She doesn't like it. / They didn't come.
- I am not ready. / He isn't here. / We aren't sure.
- I can't do it. / You shouldn't go. / I won't forget.

**部分否定 vs 全部否定：**
- Not all students passed.（不是所有人都过了 = 部分人过了）
- None of them passed.（全部没过）
- I don't always agree.（我不是每次都同意）
- I never agree.（我从不同意）

**口语中的否定缩略：**
- don't, doesn't, didn't, can't, won't, shouldn't, wouldn't, isn't, aren't, hasn't, haven't

---

## 第五部分：从句（让句子更复杂）

### 17. 名词性从句（充当主语/宾语）

**宾语从句（最常用）：**
- I think (that) he is right.
- I don't know where she went.
- Can you tell me what time it is?
- I wonder if he will come.

**口语要点：** that 通常省略；语序用陈述句语序（不倒装）：
- ✓ Do you know where he is?
- ✗ ~~Do you know where is he?~~

**主语从句：**
- What he said surprised me.
- It is clear that she is lying.（it 形式主语更常用）

---

### 18. 定语从句（修饰名词）

**关系代词：who（人）, which（物）, that（人/物）, whose（谁的）**

| 类型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 修饰人 | The man who/that helped me was very kind. |
| 修饰物 | The book which/that I bought is interesting. |
| 所有格 | The girl whose father is a doctor. |
| 地点 | The restaurant where we had dinner. |
| 时间 | The day when I first met you. |

**口语中：**
- that 比 who/which 更常用
- 作宾语时可以省略：The movie (that) I watched was great.
- 介词不放前面：The person I talked to（不说 to whom I talked）

---

### 19. 状语从句

**时间从句：**
- When I got home, she was sleeping.
- Before you leave, call me.
- After I finish work, I will go.
- While I was cooking, he was watching TV.
- As soon as I arrive, I will text you.
- I have lived here since 2020.

**条件从句：**
- If it rains, I will stay home.
- If you need help, just ask.
- Unless you hurry, we will be late.（unless = if not）

**原因从句：**
- I stayed home because I was sick.
- Since you are here, let's start.

**让步从句：**
- Although/Even though it was cold, we went out.
- No matter what he says, don't believe him.

**结果从句：**
- It was so hot that I couldn't sleep.
- He is such a nice person that everyone likes him.

**目的从句：**
- I got up early so that I could catch the train.
- I spoke slowly so that he could understand.

## 第六部分：动词的特殊用法

### 20. 不定式 (to do)

**作宾语：**
- I want to go home.
- She decided to quit.
- He promised to help me.
- 常接 to do 的动词：want, need, plan, decide, hope, promise, agree, refuse, learn, try

**作目的状语：**
- I went to the store to buy milk.
- She called to ask about the meeting.

**作宾语补足语：**
- I asked him to wait.
- She told me to be quiet.
- My mom wants me to study harder.

---

### 21. 动名词 (doing)

**作宾语：**
- I enjoy reading.
- She finished cooking.
- I avoid eating late at night.
- 常接 doing 的动词：enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest, practice, keep, consider, quit

**作主语：**
- Swimming is good exercise.
- Learning English takes time.

**介词后面用 doing：**
- I am good at cooking.
- Thank you for helping me.
- I am interested in learning Japanese.
- I am tired of waiting.

**to do vs doing 意思不同的动词：**
- stop to do（停下来去做另一件事）vs stop doing（停止正在做的事）
- remember to do（记得要做）vs remember doing（记得做过了）
- try to do（努力去做）vs try doing（试着做做看）

---

### 22. 使役动词和感官动词

**使役动词（让某人做某事）：**
- make + sb + do：He made me laugh.（强迫/导致）
- let + sb + do：Let me help you.（允许）
- have + sb + do：I had him fix my computer.（安排/请）
- get + sb + to do：I got him to agree.（说服）

**感官动词：**
- see/hear/watch/feel + sb + do（完整过程）：I saw him leave.
- see/hear/watch/feel + sb + doing（正在进行）：I saw him leaving.

---

## 第七部分：其他重要语法

### 23. 比较级和最高级

**构成规则：**
- 短词：tall → taller → tallest / big → bigger → biggest
- 长词：beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
- 不规则：good → better → best / bad → worse → worst / far → farther → farthest

**用法：**
- 比较：A is + 比较级 + than B
  - She is taller than me.
  - This one is more expensive than that one.
  - He runs faster than I do.

- 最高级：the + 最高级 + in/of
  - He is the tallest in our class.
  - This is the best movie I have ever seen.

- 同级比较：as ... as
  - She is as tall as her mother.
  - I can't run as fast as you.

---

### 24. 冠词 (a / an / the)

**a / an（泛指，第一次提到）：**
- I saw a dog.（不确定哪一只）
- She is an engineer.（职业）
- I need a cup of coffee.

**the（特指，双方都知道是哪个）：**
- I saw a dog. The dog was big.（第二次提到）
- Please close the door.（房间里就那一扇）
- The sun / the moon / the earth（独一无二）

**不用冠词：**
- 泛指复数/不可数：I like dogs. / Water is important.
- 三餐：have breakfast/lunch/dinner
- 运动：play basketball / play tennis
- 语言：speak English / learn Chinese

---

### 25. 介词

**时间介词：**
- at：at 8 o'clock / at night / at noon / at the weekend
- in：in the morning / in May / in 2024 / in winter / in 5 minutes
- on：on Monday / on May 1st / on a rainy day

**地点介词：**
- at：at home / at school / at the station / at the door
- in：in the room / in China / in the car / in bed
- on：on the table / on the wall / on the bus / on the 3rd floor

**方向/运动：**
- to：go to school / walk to the park
- from：come from China / far from here
- into：walk into the room
- out of：get out of the car
- through：walk through the park

**其他高频介词搭配：**
- look at / listen to / wait for / think about / talk about
- depend on / agree with / worry about / belong to
- be good at / be interested in / be afraid of / be sorry for

---

### 26. 连词

**并列连词（连接同等成分）：**
- and：I like tea and coffee.
- but：He is smart but lazy.
- or：Do you want tea or coffee?
- so：I was tired, so I went to bed.

**关联连词：**
- both...and：Both he and I are students.
- either...or：Either you or he is wrong.
- neither...nor：Neither she nor I was invited.
- not only...but also：He is not only smart but also hardworking.

---

### 27. 代词

**人称代词：**

| | 主格 | 宾格 | 形容词性物主 | 名词性物主 | 反身 |
|--|------|------|-------------|-----------|------|
| 我 | I | me | my | mine | myself |
| 你 | you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| 他 | he | him | his | his | himself |
| 她 | she | her | her | hers | herself |
| 它 | it | it | its | — | itself |
| 我们 | we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| 他们 | they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

**不定代词（口语高频）：**
- something / anything / nothing / everything
- someone / anyone / no one / everyone
- some（肯定句）/ any（否定/疑问）
  - I need some help. / Do you have any questions? / I don't have any money.

---

### 28. There be 句型

**表示"某处有某物"：**
- There is a book on the table.
- There are many people in the park.
- There was an accident yesterday.
- There will be a meeting tomorrow.

**和 have 的区别：**
- There is（客观存在）：There is a hospital near here.
- I have（主观拥有）：I have a car.

---

## 第八部分：口语高频句式

### 29. It 句型

- It is + adj + to do：It is easy to learn.（做某事怎么样）
- It takes + 时间 + to do：It takes 2 hours to get there.（花多长时间）
- It seems/looks like：It seems like he is angry.（看起来）
- It doesn't matter：It doesn't matter, forget it.（没关系）

---

### 30. 常用口语结构

**used to / be used to / get used to：**
- I used to smoke.（过去常常，现在不了）
- I am used to getting up early.（已经习惯了）
- I am getting used to the weather here.（正在适应）

**had better（最好）：**
- You had better leave now.
- You'd better not be late.

**would like / would love：**
- I would like a cup of tea.（我想要）
- I would love to go.（我很愿意）

**How about / What about：**
- How about going to a movie?
- What about tomorrow?

**be supposed to（应该/本应）：**
- You are supposed to be here at 9.（你应该9点到）
- I was supposed to call her.（我本应该打给她的）

**tend to（倾向于）：**
- I tend to forget things.
- It tends to rain in April.

---

### 31. 强调和倒装（口语中偶尔用到）

**强调：**
- I do like it!（我确实喜欢）
- She did come yesterday.（她确实来了）
- It is you who should apologize.（该道歉的是你）

**倒装（口语中常见的几种）：**
- So do I. / Neither do I.（我也是/我也不）
- Here comes the bus.（车来了）
- Not until yesterday did I know.（直到昨天我才知道）

---

### 32. 直接引语和间接引语

**直接引语：** He said, "I am busy."

**间接引语（转述）：** He said (that) he was busy.

**变化规则：**
| 直接 | 间接 |
|------|------|
| am/is | was |
| are | were |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day |
| here | there |

**口语中常见的转述：**
- She told me she would be late.
- He asked me where I lived.
- They said they couldn't come.

---

## 第九部分：容易混淆的点

### 33. 易混词/结构对比

| 混淆点 | 区别 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| in / after | in = 从现在起多久后；after = 过去某点之后 | I will be back in 5 minutes. / He left after lunch. |
| ago / before | ago = 从现在往前；before = 从过去往前 | I came here 3 years ago. / I had seen him before. |
| already / yet | already = 肯定句；yet = 否定/疑问 | I have already eaten. / Have you eaten yet? |
| too / either | too = 肯定句末；either = 否定句末 | I like it too. / I don't like it either. |
| so / such | so + adj；such + (adj) + n | It is so hot! / It is such a hot day! |
| speak / say / tell / talk | speak 语言；say 内容；tell 某人；talk 交谈 | She speaks English. / He said goodbye. / Tell me a story. / Let's talk. |
| look / see / watch | look 看向；see 看见；watch 观看 | Look at this! / I can see the mountain. / I watch TV every night. |
| listen / hear | listen 去听；hear 听见 | Listen to me. / I can hear music. |
| borrow / lend | borrow 借入；lend 借出 | Can I borrow your pen? / Can you lend me your pen? |

### 34. 口语中的省略和缩略

**常见缩略形式：**
- I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're
- I've, you've, we've, they've
- I'll, you'll, he'll, she'll, we'll, they'll
- I'd (= I would / I had), you'd, he'd
- don't, doesn't, didn't, can't, won't, shouldn't, couldn't, wouldn't
- gonna = going to, wanna = want to, gotta = got to
- kinda = kind of, lemme = let me, gimme = give me

**口语省略：**
- (Do you) Want some coffee?
- (I am) Sorry about that.
- (It) Sounds good.
- (I have) Got to go.
